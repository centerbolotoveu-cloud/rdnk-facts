Privacy policy — RDNK Claude

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

RDNK Claude is an internal tool of the RDNK facts video channel. It is used only by the channel team, with the channel's own Google account (rdnksocial@gmail.com).

What data it uses

With the account owner's permission it accesses the channel's Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Drive: the content plan, video files and channel mail.

How the data is used

Only to plan, prepare and publish the channel's own videos. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Access tokens are kept only on the team's own computers.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
